APPLICATION INFORMATION

The Unknown Files n8n

This is the information site for our owner-operated content production and publishing app. It is not a public account-management service, and visitors cannot sign in or connect accounts here.

What the app is for

The app supports science-topic research, script and visual planning, narration, editing, review and publishing to the channel owner's connected YouTube and Instagram accounts. Some production stages are still under development; this page is not a claim that unattended daily publishing is live.

How Google access is used

When the owner authorizes Google access, the app can identify the connected YouTube channel, read its publication metadata and upload approved videos. Separately authorized Google Cloud access supports the configured video-generation services. Google permissions are not granted by visiting this website.

Our editorial direction

One useful question, a visible explanation and an honest payoff. Alice and Oliver are fictional narration identities. Illustrative animation should be distinguished from observational evidence; examples and sources need review before publication.
