Privacy policy

This policy describes The Unknown Files n8n, an owner-operated app used to support the channel's content workflow. The public information site has no account-connection form.

Operator and contact

The app is operated by the owner of The Unknown Files. Privacy and support contact: www.nitin156@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, access tokens or private keys.

Information used by the app

For accounts explicitly connected by the operator, the app processes account/channel identifiers, granted authorization scopes, access and refresh tokens, publication metadata, video files, titles, descriptions, captions and processing/upload results. Instagram integration may access professional-account information and the publishing/insights data available under its authorized permissions. Operational records include job status, review records, error codes and publishing identifiers. Public scientific sources and public trend signals may be used during research.

Purposes

Data is used to identify the correct connected accounts, research and prepare content, review assets, upload approved content, prevent duplicate publication, maintain authorized connections and notify the operator of failures. The app does not provide a service for unrelated visitors to connect their accounts.

Google API data

Google and YouTube data is accessed only under the permissions granted to the app. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising targeting or used to train generalized AI/ML models. Authorization credentials are not provided to a script-writing or visual-planning model as prompt data.

Services and disclosure

The workflow uses self-hosted n8n and storage on an Oracle-hosted server, Google/YouTube APIs, Instagram/Meta APIs and configured production or narration services. Research and production material supplied to an AI provider is processed under that provider's applicable terms. Public source text, planned scripts and production instructions can be supplied for drafting. Publishing sends the approved media and platform-specific metadata to the selected platform. The information site's hosting provider may process ordinary request data to operate and secure hosting.

Storage and retention

n8n-managed credentials and restricted server token files are used for authorization. Access is limited to the operator and authorized workflow processes. Media, job records and backups are retained as needed for production, reconciliation, troubleshooting and recovery, until removed by the operator. There is no promise of automatic deletion after a fixed number of days. Backups and platform-held copies may persist separately.

Access removal and deletion requests

The operator can disconnect credentials in n8n and revoke permissions in the connected Google or Meta account's app-access settings. For removal of retained app data, email the contact above and identify the connected account without sending secrets. Identity/authority may need to be confirmed. Removing local data does not automatically remove videos already published on YouTube or Instagram; published posts require separate removal on the relevant platform.

This website

These homepage and policy pages have no application-level login, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, contact form or visitor-upload feature. It does not itself request OAuth permissions. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may log request information for security and operations under GitHub's privacy statement. Other existing resource pages may have separate functionality; this policy describes the channel publishing app and these information pages.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies the current version.
